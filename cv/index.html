Summary

Infrastructure architect with 19 years of experience across software, networks, and operations. I design, build, and run the platforms an ISP depends on, from routing and anycast to RADIUS, VoIP, and the underlying infrastructure-as-code. I favour simple designs where a few rules produce the behaviour you want, and I contribute back to the open-source tools I rely on, including FreeSWITCH.

Employment History

July 2014 – Present
Head of InfrastructureOrbital Net Ltd · Canterbury, Kent

Joined as 3rd line support and started automating away repeat support calls from day one; promoted to Infrastructure Manager in 2019 and Head of Infrastructure in 2023. Now architect for the group’s core platforms, with full autonomy over my areas and significant influence on the team’s technical direction. Built the self-hosted VMware platform the business runs on. Selected and deployed the netElastic vBNG platform, using QinQ tag ranges to avoid per-subscriber provisioning; since extended with CGNAT, IPv6 and VXLAN to bring in other access networks, across multiple 100GbE nodes. Designed and built a custom Python/asyncio RADIUS platform behind it, replacing FreeRADIUS, horizontally scaled over RabbitMQ RPC and serving 22,000 concurrent sessions with a multi-vendor NAS driver model. Architecting a greenfield FreeSWITCH VoIP platform using RabbitMQ Streams and online fraud detection. Cloudflare Zero Trust with Entra ID and DNS managed as code with OpenTofu and peer-reviewed GitLab CI/CD pipelines. Built a staged-rollout engine for changes across field radio equipment, with controlled batches to limit blast radius, live pause/resume and a watchdog for stalled rollouts. Developing internal Django tools and handling technical escalations for customers and sales.
