Summary

Infrastructure architect with 18 years of experience across software, networks, and operations. I design, build, and run the platforms an ISP depends on, from routing and anycast to RADIUS, VoIP, and the infrastructure as code underneath. I favour simple designs where a few rules produce the behaviour you want, and I contribute back to the open-source tools I rely on, including FreeSWITCH.
